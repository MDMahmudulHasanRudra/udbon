About Udbon

A Bangladeshi software company building for Bangladeshi businesses

Most business software is made for other markets, priced in dollars and written in a language your customers don't speak. We started Udbon to change that.

We build tools for the shops, brands and teams that sell on Facebook and WhatsApp, take payment by bKash and ship by courier. Every product we make speaks Bangla, is priced in Taka, and connects to the services businesses here already use.

Our mission

Make world-class business software simple and affordable for every business in Bangladesh, from a one-person Facebook shop to a multi-brand company.

Our vision

A Bangladesh where a local shop runs on software as good as any global brand, and grows just as far.

The meaning of our name

Udbon comes from the Bangla word for a new beginning. That is what we want to give every business that works with us: a fresh start with better tools.
